SHOWTIME

EVELYN HA VLOG 
• I enjoy the relatable, comforting, and real-life everyday moments shared in the videos.

• I am drawn to the sweet high school romance and the intense love triangle.

THE WONDERFOOLS
• I enjoy the unique and funny twist on ordinary townspeople suddenly getting clumsy superpowers.
• The late-1990s nostalgia and the strong sense of friendship make the show very comforting to me.
• I love seeing regular, everyday outcasts band together to save their peaceful town.

WONDERFUL NIGHTMARE 
• I love the hilarious and chaotic concept of a body-swap comedy.
• I find it highly entertaining to watch a successful professional suddenly forced to live a completely different life.
• The story has a lot of heart and reminds me of the true value of family and love.
